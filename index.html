BillSync Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

BillSync is a personal finance tracker and bill-splitter for Android and iPhone. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. It is written to match what the app actually does. If anything here is unclear, email billsync.co@gmail.com.

BillSync is operated by an individual developer (the “data controller” for the purposes of UK and EU data protection law), reachable at the address above.

The AI assistant runs on your phone. BillSync Plus includes a chat assistant powered by an on-device model. Your questions, its answers, and the summary of your finances it reads to answer you are all processed on your phone. BillSync makes no network request for artificial intelligence, and no model provider — not us, not Google — ever receives your questions or your financial data. The only related transfer is a one-time download of the model file itself, which carries none of your data.

What data BillSync handles

Data you provide

  • Account details: your email address, and a display name. Other people find your BillSync account by typing your exact email address, which is how friend requests reach you. If you sign in with Google, Google gives BillSync a one-time identity token to establish your account, and your Google display name and profile photo are copied to your BillSync profile. If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives BillSync a one-time identity token and your email address, or a private relay address if you choose to hide your email (then only someone who knows that relay address can find you), and, the first time only, your name, which becomes your display name unless you have already chosen one. If no name comes with your sign-in, your display name starts as the part of your email address before the “@”, which the people who can see your name will see; for a hidden Apple address that part is enough to find you, so give yourself a name in Profile. You may optionally add a profile photo of your own and choose a display currency. Your accent colour is saved on your device only and never reaches our servers.
  • Your financial entries: the expenses, income, budgets, shared bills, splits and settlements you record, and the friends and groups you create. You type these in yourself, dictate them to the assistant, or photograph a receipt for it to read, and nothing is saved until you confirm it. BillSync never connects to your bank and never asks for bank credentials.

Data collected automatically

  • A push notification token for your device, and a per-installation identifier created by Google’s messaging service, so the app can send you notifications (a friend request, being added to a bill or a group, a bill declined or removed, a settlement, a reminder about money owed). On an iPhone, Apple’s push service delivers them.
  • Subscription information if you buy BillSync Plus: your BillSync account identifier goes to Google Play or the App Store with the purchase, and the purchase token or transaction identifier, the plan, its status, renewal state and expiry, together with the record the store returns about the subscription, are stored so the app knows whether Plus is active. The App Store keeps one subscription per Apple ID: if Plus is bought on the same Apple ID from a second BillSync account, the subscription moves to the account that made the latest purchase, and we keep a record of that move with the purchase record.

BillSync contains no analytics or crash-reporting software, and does not build an advertising profile of you.

How your data is stored

Your account data — transactions, budgets, friends, groups, bills and settlements — is stored in BillSync’s backend, a database hosted on Supabase in the European Union, so that it syncs across your sessions and so that friends can split bills with you. Access is restricted at the row level: nobody can read a bill they are not part of. Data is encrypted in transit. Profile photos are stored in a public bucket, which means anyone who has the photo’s web address can view it; do not upload a photo you would not want seen.

Why we use your data

  • To provide BillSync to you (the contract between us): your account, your entries, shared bills with your friends, notifications, and Plus if you buy it.
  • To keep it secure and fair (our legitimate interests): limiting how often requests can be made, checking purchases with the store, and stopping one purchase from being used by several accounts.
  • To keep purchase records after an account closes (our legitimate interests): a store can present the same purchase again at any time, through a restore, a renewal or a refund, and the record is what stops it being claimed by another account, what lets us answer a billing question with the store, and what shows when an App Store subscription moved between accounts. You can object to this, as to any processing based on our legitimate interests, by contacting us.

Your phone asks before BillSync can show notifications or use the camera, and you can turn either off in your phone’s settings at any time. Turning notifications off stops your phone showing them, but your device stays registered while you are signed in, so notifications are still sent to it through the services above; signing out removes the registration. A photo from your library is read only when you pick it.

What other people can see

When you split a bill or share a group, the friends and group members involved see your display name and avatar, the bills you share with them, and who has paid what. Your display name and photo can also be seen by anyone who has sent you a friend request, or to whom you have sent one, before it is accepted. Nobody can see your email address, except where your display name was taken from it (see Account details above) and you have not changed it. Members of a group also see the group’s total spent, which can include bills they are not personally on, but never those bills’ titles or the people on them.

The on-device AI, in detail

The assistant uses Google’s Gemma model, which downloads once (about 2.6 GB) and then stays on your device. When you ask it something, the app assembles a summary of your finances from the same account data described above and runs the model locally to produce an answer. Nothing you ask, and nothing it reads, is sent anywhere for processing. Any action you approve — logging an expense, splitting a bill, recording a settlement — is saved to your account exactly as if you had entered it by hand. The conversation is never uploaded and is never written to storage: it is held in memory only, and is discarded when you sign out, switch account, or close the app.

The assistant can also read a photo of a receipt. You take or choose the photo, the app shrinks it, and the model reads it on your phone to suggest an entry for you to check. The photo is never uploaded and BillSync does not save it: it is held in memory with the conversation. Your phone’s camera and photo picker may leave a temporary copy in BillSync’s own cache on the device, which never leaves your phone.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your personal expenses and income, your budgets, your friendships and your group memberships are removed, your sign-in and email address are deleted, and your name is replaced with the placeholder “Deleted User” on any shared bills your friends keep, so their own records stay intact. Your profile photo is deleted from our storage at the same time; if that step fails, email us and we will remove it. A record of your purchases — the store’s purchase token or transaction identifier, the plan and the dates, the record the store returned about the subscription, and any move of an App Store subscription between accounts — is kept afterwards, for the reasons under Why we use your data above, and is no longer linked to your name or email address, with two exceptions: a record of a move names both accounts, and if Plus is later bought on the same Apple ID from another BillSync account, that subscription and its purchase record move to that account. Deletion takes effect in BillSync’s live systems immediately, apart from short-lived operational logs. Our database provider may hold short-lived operational backups of the database, which are overwritten on the provider’s own schedule; BillSync does not use them to restore individual accounts.

Request account deletion

You can delete your BillSync account and its data yourself, in the app: open Profile → Close account, at the bottom of the Profile tab, and confirm (in earlier versions of the app, Settings → Close account). You do not need the app to make the request — email billsync.co@gmail.com from the address on your BillSync account, ask for your account to be deleted, and we will delete it and confirm by reply.

Two things must be true before an account can be closed, and BillSync says so if they are not:

  • Every shared bill between you and your friends is settled.
  • Your BillSync Plus subscription has stopped billing. A subscription that is active, in its grace period, on hold or paused must first be cancelled in Google Play or the App Store, wherever you bought it. A subscription you have already cancelled does not block deletion, even if its paid period has not run out; the remainder of that period is lost when the account goes.

What deletion removes, and the purchase record it keeps, are described under How long data is kept above. If you signed in with Apple, you can also stop BillSync using your Apple ID: on an iPhone, open your Apple ID settings, find Sign in with Apple, choose BillSync and stop using it.

Your rights

Because your data is held in the EU, UK and EU data protection law applies. You have the right to:

  • Access the data BillSync holds about you.
  • Correct it — most of your data you can edit directly in the app.
  • Delete your account and data, from your Profile (subject to the conditions above), or by asking us.
  • Receive a copy of your data, or object to its processing, by contacting us.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office).

To exercise any of these, email billsync.co@gmail.com.

Children

BillSync is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Your bills, splits, settlements, budgets and personal transactions are readable only by you and the people who are on them, enforced by row-level security in the database. Your display name and profile photo are deliberately visible to your friends and group members, as described above, and a profile photo you upload is served from a public address. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.
