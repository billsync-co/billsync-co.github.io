BillSync Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

BillSync is a personal finance tracker and bill-splitter for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. It is written to match what the app actually does. If anything here is unclear, email billsync.co@gmail.com.

BillSync is operated by an individual developer (the “data controller” for the purposes of UK and EU data protection law), reachable at the address above.

The AI assistant runs on your phone. BillSync Plus includes a chat assistant powered by an on-device model. Your questions, its answers, and the summary of your finances it reads to answer you are all processed on your phone. BillSync makes no network request for artificial intelligence, and no model provider — not us, not Google — ever receives your questions or your financial data. The only related transfer is a one-time download of the model file itself, which carries none of your data.

What data BillSync handles

Data you provide

  • Account details: your email address, and a display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive a Google identity token to establish your account. You may optionally add a profile photo and choose a display currency and an accent colour.
  • Your financial entries: the expenses, income, budgets, shared bills, splits and settlements you record, and the friends and groups you create. You type these in yourself, or dictate them to the assistant for your confirmation. BillSync never connects to your bank and never asks for bank credentials.

Data collected automatically

  • A push notification token for your device, so the app can send you notifications (a friend request, being added to a bill, a settlement, a group invite).
  • Subscription information if you buy BillSync Plus: your BillSync account identifier and the purchase are handled through Google Play, and the subscription’s status and expiry are stored so the app knows whether Plus is active.

BillSync contains no analytics or crash-reporting software, and does not build an advertising profile of you.

How your data is stored

Your account data — transactions, budgets, friends, groups, bills and settlements — is stored in BillSync’s backend, a database hosted on Supabase in the European Union, so that it syncs across your sessions and so that friends can split bills with you. Access is restricted at the row level: nobody can read a bill they are not part of. Data is encrypted in transit. Profile photos are stored in a public bucket, which means anyone who has the photo’s web address can view it; do not upload a photo you would not want seen.

What other people can see

When you split a bill or share a group, the friends and group members involved see your display name and avatar, the bills you share with them, and who has paid what. Members of a group also see the group’s total spent, which can include bills they are not personally on, but never those bills’ titles or the people on them.

The on-device AI, in detail

The assistant uses Google’s Gemma model, which downloads once (about 2.6 GB) and then stays on your device. When you ask it something, the app assembles a summary of your finances from the same account data described above and runs the model locally to produce an answer. Nothing you ask, and nothing it reads, is sent anywhere for processing. Any action you approve — logging an expense, splitting a bill, recording a settlement — is saved to your account exactly as if you had entered it by hand. The conversation itself is not stored; closing the chat discards it.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account (from the app’s Settings, once your balances are settled and any Plus subscription is cancelled), your personal data — your transactions, budgets, friendships and group memberships — is removed, and your name is replaced with an anonymised placeholder on any shared bills your friends keep, so their own records stay intact. Anonymised billing and subscription records are retained afterwards as an accounting and audit trail. Deleted data may persist in encrypted operational backups for a limited period (typically up to 30 days) before those backups are overwritten.

Your rights

Because your data is held in the EU, UK and EU data protection law applies. You have the right to:

  • Access the data BillSync holds about you.
  • Correct it — most of your data you can edit directly in the app.
  • Delete your account and data, from Settings (subject to the conditions above), or by asking us.
  • Receive a copy of your data, or object to its processing, by contacting us.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office).

To exercise any of these, email billsync.co@gmail.com.

Children

BillSync is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to your records is enforced at the database level so that only you and the people you share bills with can read them. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, note them here. Continued use of BillSync after a change means you accept the updated policy.
